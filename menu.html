Feteer Meshaltet

فطير مشلتت

Thirty layers, thrown by hand and folded with clarified butter, baked nine minutes on bare stone. Served with wild honey and clotted cream — or stuffed with rumi cheese and basterma if the table is hungry.

Allow twenty-five minutes. It cannot be hurried, and we will not try.
